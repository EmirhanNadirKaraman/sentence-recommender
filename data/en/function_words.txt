# Function words — the closed word classes of English.
#
# Every line here counts as ALREADY KNOWN when the roadmap decides which
# sentences are i+1. These are the classes a learner acquires wholesale
# rather than one item at a time: articles and determiners, pronouns,
# prepositions, conjunctions, auxiliaries and modals, and the handful of
# particles and question words every sentence is built on.
#
# Written by hand rather than read off the corpus: German's list is the
# closed-class tags of its tagset, and English's tags (Penn) do not separate
# the classes the same way. Each entry is resolved through the English parser
# like any other word, so `me` and `I` meet in one lemma.
#
# HOW TO REVIEW
#   Delete a line — or put a `#` in front of it — for anything you do NOT
#   know. What remains is treated as known. Order does not matter.
