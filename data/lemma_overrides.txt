# German drops the -e of the first person and the imperative as soon as anyone
# is speaking rather than writing: habe/sage/gehe/mache/höre/lasse become hab,
# sag, geh, mach, hör, lass. spaCy's lemma table is built from written
# wordlists that carry the full forms and none of the clipped ones, and its
# lemmatiser leaves what it cannot place alone — so every one of these came
# through as a unit of its own, sitting beside the infinitive the reader
# already knows.
#
# 2,454 subtitle sentences hold one. 255 of them have no other unknown at all,
# so they are unreadable for no reason but this.

hab     haben
lass    lassen
sag     sagen
hör     hören
mach    machen
geh     gehen
